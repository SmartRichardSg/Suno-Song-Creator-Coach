ℹ️ How this app works

This app does not generate music itself. It helps you prepare a good brief for an AI assistant, then organize the result for use in Suno.

Exact duration, fade-out length and silence are not guaranteed by the AI or Suno. If the ending is abrupt, use Suno's editing tools where available to adjust the ending.
